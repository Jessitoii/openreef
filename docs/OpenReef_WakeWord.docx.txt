Without VAD, either a fixed timeout (bad UX) or manual button press is needed.VAD algorithm (simple energy-based, on-device):  Record audio in 30ms chunks  Calculate RMS energy of each chunk  Speaking threshold: RMS > 0.02  Silence threshold:  RMS < 0.01 for 500ms → stop recording  Max recording: 30 seconds (prevent runaway)Result: natural conversation feel — user speaks, pauses, STT fires.Alternative: Silero VAD (LiteRT) — more accurate, ~2MB model.Can be added in v1.1 if energy-based VAD proves insufficient.▸ F.5 — User SettingsSettingDefaultOptionsWake word phrase"hey openreef""hey reef" · "hey lobster" · custom (v1.1)Wake word enabledOFFUser explicitly enables — battery transparencySensitivity0.70.3 (fewer false positives) → 0.9 (more responsive)Voice response (TTS)ONON / OFF / Headphones onlySTT languageAuto-detectAny of the 7 supported languagesTTS voice engineAndroid built-inAndroid built-in · Kokoro (download required)Silence timeout500ms300ms / 500ms / 1000msWake word while screen offONON / OFF▸ F.6 — Battery OptimizationThe most common objection to always-on microphone is battery drain.
